# 紙飛機穿城 n03 交件紀錄

## n03a.png

實際英文生成 prompt： Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final concept artwork for an infinite forward-flight desktop wallpaper. Generate one finished landscape image at 1920x1080, exact 16:9 composition. Primary request: a single origami paper airplane flying forward into the depth of an endless modern white paper architectural model city. The positive visual reference is a dense monochrome architectural massing-model photograph with rectilinear towers of varied heights, deep urban street canyons, layered white-on-white volumes and atmospheric distance. Style/medium: refined architectural model photography / physically based white-model visualization. Every building, road, pedestrian bridge, viaduct and utility structure is made of white or very pale ivory paper or card. Clean contemporary urban massing, not little houses. Show subtle paper fibers, precise folds, faintly imperfect cut corners and believable visible card thickness at selected near edges, without turning the scene into crumpled paper. Use a restrained number of facade slots and model details. Realistic soft daylight, readable cast shadows, rich but delicate ambient occlusion, white surfaces separated by light and shadow. Composition/framing: one identifiable folded paper airplane, seen from the rear and slightly to one side, nose pointing away into the vanishing point, banking gently. The airplane occupies about 1/6 of image height, not an oversized hero, and flies through the canyon at about mid-building height. Keep sharp focal clarity on the airplane and the central midground. Strong forward perspective, canyon lines continuing beyond the visible scene; near foreground tower edges, midground city, far city dissolving into atmospheric haze and pale sky must read as distinct layers. Very slight passing motion softness at only the extreme foreground edges. Leave the upper-right 25 percent of width and upper 22 percent of height as clean quiet pale sky/haze for a later HUD, without drawing a box or any UI. Color palette: over 90 percent neutral white, light grey or faint ivory. Exactly one restrained accent family as specified below. Sky pale grey-white or almost-white paper tone. Avoid: colorful storybook town, cartoon, illustration outlines, cute cottages, pitched red roofs, bright blue sky, turquoise canal, vegetation, birds, colorful props, glossy plastic, metal city, dark asphalt, black window grids, gritty concrete, oversaturated colors, broad sepia wash, text, letters, logo, watermark, UI, HUD, border, collage or multiple panels. Variant n03a: afternoon sunlight. A pure white modern paper city, with neutral softly directional afternoon sunlight from upper left, long clear delicate grey shadows and moderate atmospheric haze. ONLY accent: the airplane itself is very pale powder-blue paper, with visible folded wings and central crease. The blue is subdued and limited entirely to the plane. Camera follows close behind and slightly to the left at mid-tower altitude, looking slightly downward along a wide straight street canyon toward a vanishing point just right of center. The city is vast, varied and believable, not a bilateral repeated corridor. White paper elevated connecting structures can cross the street well below the flying plane. 實際英文修訂 prompt（以該張初稿為唯一輸入）： Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied concept image with one localized change only: reduce the existing origami airplane uniformly to 72 percent of its current width and height, keeping its center at exactly the same position. Preserve the airplane design, folds, orientation and original paper color. Reconstruct the small revealed background naturally to match the existing buildings. Keep the entire city, camera angle, lighting, shadows, fog, color palette, paper textures, foreground motion softness and clean upper-right sky unchanged. The final airplane should occupy approximately 15 to 16 percent of total image height. Keep a single airplane only, no extra objects, no text, no UI, no watermark. Deliver one full landscape image, 16:9. 即時 3D 難點：在無限串流的城市模組上維持紙纖維尺度、紙板邊厚、柔和陰影與環境遮蔽的一致性，同時避免近景快速掠過時閃爍。
