# 無限系列桌布概念圖

## x03.png｜土星環滑行

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a single 16:9 landscape concept illustration for an infinite-motion Windows wallpaper, intended for 1920 × 1080.
Art direction: Japanese animation background art, softly realistic matte color, clean silhouettes, lightly textured painted surfaces and clear atmospheric perspective. Follow a small protagonist with a low rear tracking camera and use flowing scene lines to pull the eye toward a vanishing point.
Scene and subject: Saturn ring surfing. A human in a pearl-gray vacuum suit with muted teal trim and a closed round helmet stands on a slim futuristic surfboard. Place the rider in the lower-left third with their full body seen from behind, about 28 percent of the frame height. Their bent knees and trailing arc of ice dust show steady forward motion.
Depth: Close, softly blurred ice chunks and fine dust ripples skim across the lower edge. The midground is a wide ring plane with layered, continuous ice bands curving and receding toward a clear vanishing point. Far behind the ring plane, an immense Saturn fills the left background, its recognizable globe in pale straw and cream with fine, subdued horizontal bands. Black space extends beyond it, with a small distant sun illuminating the icy rings from near the left horizon. Use warm straw, ochre highlights, and silvery charcoal.
Brief event: A small artificial satellite with recognizable solar panels passes low above the midground ring plane, casting a visible shadow across the sunlit ice bands. It is a secondary detail.
Leave a broad area of clean, dark space in the upper right for a future HUD. No lettering, text, logos, UI, or watermark.
```

做成即時 3D 桌布時，最難的是讓近景冰塊、冰塵與環帶紋理隨速度無縫接續，並讓掠過的衛星陰影穩定地落在環面上。

## x04.png｜太空電梯攀升

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a single 16:9 landscape concept illustration for an infinite-motion Windows wallpaper, intended for 1920 × 1080.
Art direction: Japanese animation background art with softly realistic matte color, warm accents on cool surfaces, crisp silhouettes, and clear atmospheric perspective. Lead the eye along a receding path across a broad scene.
Scene and subject: Space elevator ascent from high above Earth. This is an ultra-wide exterior tracking shot from slightly above and to the side of a compact ascending elevator capsule, showing its roof and side, with opaque blue-tinted windows and porcelain-white panels. Place the entire main capsule around the lower-left third and make it exactly one quarter of the full image height. Leave space to see the cable and the landscape around it. The parallel guide rails of its tether run from the lower left upward to a vanishing point above the center-left and continue into space.
Depth: Close guide-rail lights skim the near left edge with restrained directional streaks. Below the capsule, several distinct tiers of soft cloud banks drift down toward Earth's distant curved horizon. The sky grades from vivid cobalt at the luminous Earth limb to almost black across the top. Glacial blue, porcelain white and a few amber rail indicators form the palette.
Brief event: A smaller descending capsule approaches on the opposing guide rail of the same tether, just above and beside the main capsule, with a short downward light streak. Keep it clearly secondary.
Leave a broad quiet area of low-contrast gradient sky in the upper right for a future HUD. No lettering, text, logos, UI, or watermark.
```

做成即時 3D 桌布時，最難的是在同一套鏡頭尺度下維持導軌與雙艙交會的精度，同時平滑過渡雲層視差與地球弧面的遠景。

## x05.png｜星際鯨洄游

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one full-bleed 16:9 landscape concept image for an infinite-motion Windows wallpaper, ideally 1920 × 1080, with the composition safe for a very slight crop if needed.
Image 1 is a style and composition reference. Carry over its Japanese animation background art with softly realistic matte color, clean silhouettes and atmospheric perspective, a modestly sized focal subject, and environmental lines that pull the eye forward. Invent entirely new scenery for this subject.
Scene: Interstellar whale migration. A pod of giant bioluminescent cosmic whales swims freely through a nebula, seen side-on with a shallow three-quarter sense of depth. The adult focal whale is in the lower-left third, roughly 27 percent frame height with its fins and tail visible. Its gently luminous flank and recognizable whale anatomy anchor the scene. The group swims rightward and deeper into the image, following broad currents that curve toward a vanishing region slightly right of center, with no literal road.
Depth: A sparse few softly streaked luminous particles shed from the whales' skin sweep past the immediate foreground. Several whales at distinct middle distances overlap loosely, with softer edges and smaller silhouettes as they recede. Far away near the center-right, a small, partially veiled newborn star glows inside its natal gas. Broad layered nebula clouds form the sky. Use deep aubergine, dusty rose, and soft lilac with gentle amber around the forming star. Favor painted gradients, restrained glow, clear silhouettes and uncluttered atmosphere.
Brief event: A visibly smaller calf trails at the left rear of the pod, angling its body and trailing a short line of luminous particles as it catches up. Keep the calf a small accent. Leave the upper-right corner quiet with low-contrast nebula haze for a future HUD. No lettering, logos, UI, or watermark.
```

做成即時 3D 桌布時，最難的是讓鯨群與追上的幼鯨自然維持隊形，同時控制星雲與發光粒子重疊造成的 GPU 負擔。
