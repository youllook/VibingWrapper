# 夢境之門｜生成紀錄

五張定稿均使用內建 ImageGen，以 `target.png` 作為畫風參考。原圖約 1,915 × 821 像素，依各張的視平線或消失點裁切置中，再以 Lanczos 重取樣為 2688 × 1152 像素的 21:9 RGB PNG。左右淡出預留給圓柱背景的渲染端。

以下為五張定稿實際送出的提示詞。

Paint the world itself as a continuous uninterrupted landscape. Exclude doors, doorframes, characters, portals and the framing elements of the reference.

```text
Daytime lighthouse coast. From a low, gently sloping shore, look across a brilliant blue and turquoise sea with crisp white breakers washing in from a rocky headland. Natural low sand and damp rock across the bottom, open water and clear air toward both sides. Place a white lighthouse upright on a middle-distance rocky promontory wholly inside the inner 65% of the frame. It should feel far enough away to look through a doorway. The uninterrupted distant sea horizon follows the exact mid-height of the image. A sunny blue sky and softly billowing cumulus above, with warm white daylight.

Deliver a very wide 21:9 horizontal panorama at 2688×1152 or another resolution at least 2400 pixels wide. Use a 16:9 wide frame if 21:9 cannot be generated. A level rectilinear wide-angle camera at 1.6 m eye height, with no pitch or roll; align true eye level and any perspective vanishing point exactly to the horizontal centerline at y = 50% of the full output height. Natural ground or a walkable surface enters at the bottom edge. Designed for a cylindrical backdrop: the outermost left and right 15% have only continuous unobstructed scenery in broad layers, without nearby trunks, posts, walls, tall objects, or conspicuous elements clipped by the sides. Both edges should be suitable for independent blending. Keep the image full bleed with natural colors out to the edges. No people, no animals, no text, no signage, no watermarks, no border or letterboxing.
```
