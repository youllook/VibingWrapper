# 環形太空站 Tripo 輸入圖

以 `target.png` 為風格參考。以下記錄內建 ImageGen 的實際提示詞。初次生成後，以各自的初版作為 Image 1 進行構圖與背景調整。

五張成品均為 1254 × 1254 PNG。ImageGen 產出的背景為近似淺灰。各節列出背景邊緣抽樣的最常見 RGB 色值，目標色為 `#D8D8D8`。物件佔比以畫面最長投影邊粗估。

## townhouses.png

物件最長邊約佔畫面 64%，背景抽樣眾數為 `#D4D4D4`。

### 初次生成

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one isolated asset reference for Tripo image-to-3D.
Style: the soft, grounded realism of a Japanese animated background from a sunlit ring station. Warm ivory surfaces, subtle sea-glass jade where appropriate, and a pale golden tint in the illumination. Realistic forms with finely controlled, quiet surface detail.
Camera and framing: square 1:1 image, at least 1024 × 1024. Slight perspective, three-quarter view, camera about 30 degrees above the object and 45 degrees off the primary view. Center the full silhouette, including any protrusions, in both axes. The object's longest extent occupies about 70 percent of the image. Leave clean margins on all sides.
Backdrop: one perfectly flat, solid, even light gray #D8D8D8 background, edge to edge, with no horizon, floor plane, gradient, vignette, ground shadow, reflection or cast shadow on the backdrop. Large soft diffuse lighting with gentle form shading, no hard shadows or blown specular highlights. One asset only. No text, symbols, labels, markings, scale bars, borders, or watermarks.
Subject: one compact, connected townhouse block for a landscaped ring-station neighborhood. Show a small arrangement of adjoining two- and three-story ivory townhouses around a small central courtyard visible from above, with one modest leafy green tree growing in that courtyard. Mix light sand or pale stone low-pitched roofs with flat roofs. Use muted jade sea-glass glazing in regularly spaced windows and subtle warm metal detailing. Make the narrow courtyard paving and planting bed integral to the building footprint and terminate the footprint neatly at the walls. No terrain slab, plinth, street, vehicles, people, or neighboring structures. Show the front and one side from above, emphasizing the clear silhouette of the single block and the courtyard.
```

### 成品調整

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset: townhouses.png, a single isolated Tripo image-to-3D asset.
Image 1 is the asset to reframe. Keep the exact same subject, silhouettes, shape, pose, construction, materials, and three-quarter viewpoint shown in Image 1. Make the full subject smaller on a 1:1 square canvas. Its longest projected extent should be 68–70% of the canvas side. Add about 15–16% clear empty space at each end of that extent. Align the midpoint of the subject's bounding box with the midpoint of the canvas. Preserve the slightly elevated camera, the soft Japanese animated background art treatment, and the existing gentle ivory and warm tones. 
Fill every pixel outside the subject with a perfectly uniform neutral light gray digital matte, sRGB (216, 216, 216), hex #D8D8D8. The backdrop is a single flat graphic color fill. No visible texture, variation, shadow, floor plane, horizon, fringe, vignette, gradients, or markings anywhere in the blank margins. Leave only this one asset on the gray matte. Use a square render at 1024×1024 or larger.
```
