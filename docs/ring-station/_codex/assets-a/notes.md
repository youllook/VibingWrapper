## 五張共用的去背 prompt

每次輸入對應建築的初稿，`transparent_background` 為 `true`。

```text
Edit the supplied single-building architectural render only to isolate its existing building on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Preserve the exact building design, geometry, viewpoint, scale and framing, outline, all fine building details, ivory and sea-glass materials, lighting and foliage. Retain the complete spire, rails, eaves and any attached plants. Maintain the rendered appearance of glass panels backed by the interior. Remove every part of the gray background and any exterior shadow. Pixels beyond the building must have zero alpha. Output a square PNG with actual transparency and a clean antialiased edge.
```
